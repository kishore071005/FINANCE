# 01 — Purpose and Core Objectives

## Purpose

Build an internal Finance Dashboard for tracking and understanding company financial activity.

The dashboard must provide a clear answer to:

> "How much money is coming in, where is the company spending money, and what payments are coming up?"

This harness is the **source of truth** for implementation.

## Core Objective

The system must track:

- Company income / revenue
- Customer invoices
- Expenses
- Employee salaries
- SaaS and recurring subscriptions
- Domains and hosting
- Vendors
- Departmental budgets
- Financial reports
- Pending payments
- Upcoming payments
- Overall financial position

The implementation must be modular, maintainable, testable, and suitable for an internal business system.

## Functional Modules (High Level)

1. Finance Overview
2. Income / Revenue
3. Expense Management
4. Employee Salary Management
5. SaaS & Subscription Management
6. Domain / Hosting Management
7. Vendor Management
8. Budget Management
9. Financial Reports

Each module has its own detailed requirements under `modules/<name>/`.
